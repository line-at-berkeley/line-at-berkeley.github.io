The legal technology landscape can be organized into several broad categories.
This taxonomy serves as a primer for students and practitioners seeking to
understand the field.

## Practice Management & Operations

Tools that help law firms and legal departments manage their workflows,
billing, client relationships, and day-to-day operations.

## Document Automation & Contract Management

Platforms that automate the drafting, review, negotiation, and lifecycle
management of legal documents and contracts.

## Legal Research & Analytics

AI-powered tools for case law research, litigation analytics, and
predictive outcomes.

## Compliance & Regulatory Technology (RegTech)

Solutions that help organizations monitor, manage, and comply with
regulatory requirements.

## Dispute Resolution & Litigation

Tools for e-discovery, case management, arbitration platforms, and
litigation support.

## Access to Justice

Platforms that expand access to legal services for underserved populations,
including self-help tools, legal marketplaces, and pro bono coordination.

## Legal Education & Knowledge Management

Platforms for legal training, continuing education, and institutional
knowledge capture.
