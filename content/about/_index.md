LINE at Berkeley is a student initiative serving the intersection of law,
technology, and entrepreneurship. Founded at UC Berkeley School of Law, LINE
connects students with the legal innovation ecosystem through events, research,
and community.

## Mission

We aim to bridge the gap between legal practice and technological innovation by
curating resources, hosting practitioners, and building a shared knowledge base
for students interested in legal technology.
