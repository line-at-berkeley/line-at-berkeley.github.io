A panel discussion featuring leaders from legal aid organizations and
civic technology nonprofits exploring how technology can bridge the
justice gap. Panelists will discuss tools for self-represented litigants,
AI-assisted legal triage, and the role of law students in building
access-to-justice technology.

Lunch will be provided.
