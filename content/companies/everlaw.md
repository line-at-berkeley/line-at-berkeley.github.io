Everlaw is a cloud-native e-discovery platform founded by UC Berkeley
computer science alumni. The platform uses AI and machine learning to
accelerate document review, prediction coding, and case strategy. It
is used by law firms, corporations, and government agencies for
litigation, investigations, and compliance matters.
