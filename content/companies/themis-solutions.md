Themis Solutions, now operating as Unity, provides compliance
management software that helps organizations meet anti-money
laundering (AML), know-your-customer (KYC), and other regulatory
requirements. The platform automates compliance workflows, risk
assessments, and reporting to help financial institutions and
regulated industries stay compliant.
