JustFix is a nonprofit civic technology organization that builds
tools to help tenants exercise their legal rights. Its products
include tools for documenting housing disrepair, sending formal
complaints to landlords, and connecting tenants with legal
resources. JustFix demonstrates how technology can expand access
to justice for underserved communities.
