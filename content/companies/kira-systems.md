Kira Systems uses machine learning to identify, extract, and analyze
provisions from contracts and other documents. Widely used in M&A
due diligence, lease abstraction, and regulatory compliance reviews,
Kira was acquired by Litera in 2021 and continues to operate as a
contract intelligence product within the Litera suite.
